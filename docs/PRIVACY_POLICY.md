# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 2024

## Overview

ReelRoom ("we", "our", "us") is a web application that enables users to watch Instagram Reels together in real-time. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Display Name**: When you create or join a room, you provide a display name. This is not linked to any account.
- **Room Data**: Room codes, settings, and chat messages are stored temporarily during active sessions.

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data**: We collect anonymous analytics about room duration, participant counts, and feature usage.
- **Technical Data**: Browser type, device type, and IP address for security and debugging purposes.

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- **Instagram Credentials**: We never ask for or store your Instagram login credentials.
- **Personal Accounts**: We do not access your Instagram, Google, or GitHub account data beyond basic profile information used for authentication.
- **Private Content**: We do not scrape, store, or access private Instagram content.

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Enable real-time room functionality
- Improve our service and user experience
- Debug issues and ensure security
- Prevent abuse and enforce our terms

## Data Storage and Security

- **Ephemeral Storage**: Chat messages and room state are stored only for the duration of an active room session
- **No Long-term Storage**: We do not permanently store chat logs or viewing history
- **Encryption**: All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/WSS
- **TURN Servers**: WebRTC connections may use TURN servers for connectivity; media is not stored

## Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services:
- **Instagram oEmbed API**: To display public Instagram content
- **Google Cloud / Vercel**: For hosting and infrastructure
- **Authentication Providers**: Google and GitHub for optional sign-in

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access**: Request information about your data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of any stored data
- **Opt-out**: Choose not to use features that require data collection

## Children's Privacy

ReelRoom is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by posting a notice on our website.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
- Email: privacy@reelroom.app

## Cookie Policy

ReelRoom uses minimal cookies:
- **Session Cookies**: To maintain your session within a room
- **Preference Cookies**: To remember your theme preference (light/dark mode)

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.
